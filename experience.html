Projects

Engineering work

01

Helping Hand

Robotic Prosthetic Hand · Electrical Hardware Lead

Helping Hand is a university-funded multidisciplinary project developing a robotic prosthetic hand. My work focuses on the electrical hardware, including the power requirements of the servo system and the circuitry supporting the control electronics.

Technical Work

  • Designing power electronics to support servo-stall current transients while maintaining logic signal integrity.
  • Using OrCAD X PSpice simulation to target voltage averages and ripple.
  • Reading datasheets and selecting electrical and mechanical components to meet design tolerances.
02

FPGA Reciprocal Cipher

Digital Logic · Verilog · FPGA

This project was an FPGA circuit that accepts a 4-bit input and a 4-bit key and produces a 4-bit cipher output. The design used several layers of reciprocal cipher logic that could be toggled and tested through the completed circuit.

Technical Work

  • Built an FPGA circuit using a 4-bit input, 4-bit key, and 4-bit cipher output.
  • Designed and implemented two-way analog-to-digital converter logic.
  • Implemented several layers of toggleable reciprocal ciphers using Verilog and Apio.
  • Used lab equipment to analyze and debug the circuit.
03

Precision Catapult

Mechanical Design · Fabrication

The Precision Catapult was a mechanical launch system designed around repeatable and calculable trajectories. The design required an adjustable launch geometry as well as a practical way to guide the system without relying on specialized manufacturing.

Technical Work

  • Designed and fabricated a mechanical launch system with repeatable and calculable trajectories.
  • Designed adjustable launch geometry from 0° to 90°.
  • Designed for full coverage within the system's maximum range.
  • Devised a two-axis guide system to replicate radial guidance without specialized manufacturing.
